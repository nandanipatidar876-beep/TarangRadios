About Tarang Radios

Trusted in Electronics. Established Since 1972.

Tarang Radios is an established electronics distribution and trading company based in Indore, with over five decades of industry experience. We specialize in electronic components, cables, connectors, relays, switches, soldering solutions, and DJ spare parts.

Backed by strong industry relationships, reliable inventory, and an experienced team, we serve dealers, retailers, technicians, and businesses across Indore and Central India with a commitment to quality, reliability, and long-term partnerships.
